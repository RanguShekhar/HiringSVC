Help Center Frequently Asked Questions
How do I submit a new candidate profile?

To submit a new candidate, navigate to the "Job Seeker" section in the sidebar. Fill out the required information in the form and click "Submit".

 Can I edit a submitted profile?

Yes, go to "Profile Management", find the candidate in the list, and click the edit icon. Make your changes and save.

 How do I search for specific skills?

Use the "Search Profile" page. Enter the skills (e.g., "Angular", "Java") in the search bar to filter candidates who match those criteria.

 Who can I contact for technical support?

For technical issues, please email our support team at support@hiringservice.com or call our helpdesk at 1-800-555-0199.
